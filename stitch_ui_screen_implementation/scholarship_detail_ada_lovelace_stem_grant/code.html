Overview & Purpose

The Ada Lovelace STEM Leadership Grant was created to support exceptional collegiate innovators who demonstrate high technical aptitude coupled with proven initiative in peer mentorship, open-source technology, or grassroots engineering education.

Recognizing the critical role diverse leadership plays in ethical artificial intelligence, software infrastructure, and computational research, the foundation disburses awards directly to academic institutions to offset full tuition, campus housing, and necessary technical hardware costs.
